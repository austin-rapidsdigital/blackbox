## What's new

- **ClamAV definitions on Linux**, checked like Defender's on Windows: the daily database version and build date (a gap after 30 days, or with no definitions loaded), and whether the ClamAV scanner service is running. Shown in the Antivirus column of Audit health, on each system's page, in `blackbox check`, and as "Antivirus definitions out of date" in the status icon. Read only; Blackbox never updates definitions.
- **Antivirus definitions may be up to 30 days old** (was 7), for both Defender and ClamAV. Older than that is flagged.
